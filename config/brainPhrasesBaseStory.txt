Jimi stands inside a high, silent apartment overlooking the Northern Agglomeration at night. Cold blue computer light falls across his pale face while the city burns far below in dirty yellow, red and cyan. He leans toward several monitors showing the interface of Nirvana. Behind him, glass, metal and almost empty furniture make the room feel uninhabited. Outside, elevated roads disappear through black fog, rain and distant electric light.
Solo stands in a narrow artificial street inside the videogame at night. He looks like an ordinary middle-aged man in worn clothes, surrounded by cheap shops, stained walls and weak electric lamps. A dead body lies nearby while familiar figures begin repeating gestures he has already seen. Solo remains still and watches them with growing fear as the nocturnal world around him continues mechanically.
A colossal electronic billboard dominates a decaying urban façade in the middle of the night. Kali fills the screen with cobalt-blue skin, long black hair, gold ornaments, multiple arms and a vivid red tongue extending from her mouth. Below her, pedestrians move through rain, steam and nocturnal street markets without looking up. Jimi crosses beneath the enormous goddess, almost disappearing among cables, umbrellas and reflections on the wet black pavement.
Inside a cramped district of illegal workshops at night, Jimi finds Joystick. The room is packed with dismantled monitors, circuit boards, wires and artificial body parts. Joystick is thin and restless, his face made unsettling by artificial eyes reflecting the surrounding screens. He holds a small optical component between his fingers while shelves around him contain technology salvaged from several different decades. Outside the dirty windows, the district remains dark except for intermittent signs and industrial lamps.
Naima sits in a dark room crowded with terminals and exposed cables late at night. Her short blue hair catches the cold light of the monitors. A small interface beside her eyebrow connects directly to a cable running toward the computer system. Her body becomes completely still while data flickers across the screens. Jimi and Joystick wait close behind her. The physical intimacy between the three is evident in their proximity, exchanged glances and familiarity: Naima has an intense sexual relationship with both men, and the same mixture of trust, desire and vulnerability that exists between their bodies also surrounds their relationship with the machine.
Later that night, Naima, Jimi and Joystick occupy a small room lit only by monitors and distant city light entering through dirty glass. Naima moves between the two men with complete familiarity, touching and holding them in turn, their bodies close and partially concealed by darkness, sheets and cables. The scene suggests a deep adult sexual intimacy between all three without separating desire from companionship. Electronic light moves across skin, artificial eyes and exposed circuitry, making human intimacy and technological presence coexist inside the same nocturnal space.
Jimi, Joystick and Naima move through a crowded night market several hours later. Their three bodies remain clearly identifiable against a dense background of food stalls, improvised clinics, old electronics and multilingual signs. Their physical familiarity remains visible in small gestures and proximity as they walk together. Steam rises between them and the buildings. Joystick leads, Naima scans the crowd and Jimi follows while looking toward another distant image of Kali glowing above the rooftops.
Jimi stands inside a dark virtual space while the physical city remains somewhere outside in the night. Lisa emerges several metres in front of him, pale skin and dark hair softly illuminated against an almost black background. She appears physically present while remaining slightly detached from the surrounding space. Jimi moves toward her as fragments of rooms, faces, Naima, Joystick and remembered nocturnal places appear faintly behind her like overlapping transparent images.
Jimi sits connected to a makeshift system inside a cheap room filled with cables and second-hand equipment during the deepest part of the night. His physical body remains motionless while the surrounding monitors display rapidly changing data. Inside his perception, the corporate network becomes an immense geometric environment of dark corridors, luminous surfaces and distant structures. He moves alone through this artificial architecture toward a protected core while the real room around his body remains almost completely dark.
Solo stands alone in the middle of his artificial street at night. The familiar buildings remain around him, but the environment has begun to fail. A wall loses its texture, a distant figure freezes, electric signs flicker and sections of the background fall into absolute darkness. Solo remains where he is and watches the world collapse around him, his tired human face calm at the centre of the unstable nocturnal image.
The Northern Agglomeration continues through the night exactly as before. Rain covers the pavement, crowds pass through markets, cables hang between old buildings and artificial eyes, implants and screens appear among ordinary bodies. High above everything, Kali still glows blue and gold on an enormous billboard. Jimi walks away beneath her, small and almost anonymous, while somewhere behind him Naima and Joystick disappear into the nocturnal crowd and the city continues without acknowledging what has disappeared.
