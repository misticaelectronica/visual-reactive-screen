A white sailing boat moves slowly across an immense blue sea beneath a bright golden sky. Corto Maltese stands alone near the bow, tall and calm in a dark sailor’s coat, one hand resting on the rigging while the wind pulls the sails above him. The water is strangely smooth, almost metallic, reflecting long paths of sunlight toward the horizon. His face is weathered by salt and distance, but his expression is serene. Ahead of him there is only open sea, light and an endless line where the world seems to begin again.
A warm storm rises over the ocean without turning the scene dark. Huge silver clouds gather above the boat while sunlight breaks through them in luminous columns. Corto holds the mast as the vessel climbs a wide green-blue wave, his coat and scarf moving violently in the wind. High above him, enormous black-winged figures glide peacefully between the clouds like mysterious guardians rather than threatening creatures. Rain shines in the sunlight, the sail stretches against the wind, and the entire sea appears alive with movement.
After the storm, the boat enters a brilliant tropical ocean under a powerful white sun. Strange floating gardens cover parts of the water: enormous leaves, coral-like plants and translucent organisms glowing orange, green and turquoise. Corto stands beneath the sail, looking toward distant islands softened by heat. The sunlight is intense enough to make the air shimmer, yet the scene remains peaceful and expansive. Everything appears to be growing, dissolving and being transformed by salt, heat and time.
Farther south, the horizon opens into an impossible ocean. Pale sirens move beneath the clear water beside the boat, while far below them the geometric ruins of an atomic Atlantis glow softly on the seabed: immense domes, broken towers and abandoned machines slowly covered by coral. Old containers, fragments of satellites and forgotten objects rest among the ruins like archaeological remains from several civilisations. Corto leans over the side of the boat and watches this submerged world pass beneath him as sunlight descends through hundreds of metres of transparent blue water.
At sunset, the sailing boat crosses a calm copper-coloured sea. Corto stands at the bow while the sky becomes gold, rose and deep blue behind him. A distant woman appears on another small boat at the horizon, almost a silhouette against the sun, while sheets of illustrated paper drift across the water around them, their black ink drawings dissolving slowly into waves. There are no bodies and no battle, only traces of adventures that may have happened or may still be waiting to happen. Corto watches the distant figure as both boats continue toward the same immense horizon.
